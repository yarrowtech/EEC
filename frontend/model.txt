# Machine Learning Model Ideas for EEC Project

## 1. Student Performance Prediction
- **Description:** Predict students' future academic performance based on historical grades, attendance, assignment submissions, and behavioral data.
- **Use Case:** Early intervention for at-risk students, personalized learning plans.
- **Input:** Grades, attendance records, assignment scores, behavioral logs.
- **Output:** Predicted grades, risk score, or performance category.

## 2. Student Dropout Risk Detection
- **Description:** Identify students at risk of dropping out using engagement, attendance, and academic data.
- **Use Case:** Alert teachers/admins to intervene early.
- **Input:** Attendance, grades, participation, complaint history.
- **Output:** Dropout risk probability or risk category.

## 3. Sentiment Analysis on Feedback/Complaints
- **Description:** Analyze the sentiment of feedback forms, complaints, and observation answers.
- **Use Case:** Detect negative trends, flag urgent issues, improve school climate.
- **Input:** Text from feedback, complaints, observation forms.
- **Output:** Sentiment score (positive/neutral/negative), urgency flag.

## 4. Behavioral Anomaly Detection
- **Description:** Detect unusual patterns in student behavior (e.g., sudden drop in participation, spikes in complaints).
- **Use Case:** Early warning for mental health or bullying issues.
- **Input:** Attendance, assignment submission times, observation answers, complaint frequency.
- **Output:** Anomaly score, alert flag.

## 5. Personalized Recommendation System
- **Description:** Recommend courses, assignments, or extracurricular activities to students based on their interests and performance.
- **Use Case:** Boost engagement, help students discover new opportunities.
- **Input:** Student profile, past course/assignment choices, grades, interests.
- **Output:** List of recommended courses/activities.

## 6. Teacher Performance Analytics
- **Description:** Analyze teacher feedback, class performance, and engagement to provide actionable insights.
- **Use Case:** Professional development, recognition, or support for teachers.
- **Input:** Student feedback, class grades, attendance, assignment completion rates.
- **Output:** Performance score, strengths/areas for improvement.

## 7. Parent Engagement Scoring
- **Description:** Score and track parent engagement based on portal activity, feedback, and meeting attendance.
- **Use Case:** Identify parents who may need more outreach or support.
- **Input:** Parent login frequency, feedback submissions, PTM attendance.
- **Output:** Engagement score, engagement category.

## 8. Automated Timetable/Exam Scheduling (Optimization)
- **Description:** Use ML/optimization to generate efficient timetables or exam schedules based on constraints.
- **Use Case:** Save admin time, avoid conflicts, maximize resource use.
- **Input:** Teacher availability, room constraints, course requirements.
- **Output:** Optimized timetable or exam schedule.

## 9. Health & Wellbeing Risk Assessment
- **Description:** Predict students at risk of health or wellbeing issues using observation answers, health reports, and attendance.
- **Use Case:** Proactive health interventions, counseling referrals.
- **Input:** Observation form answers, health report data, attendance.
- **Output:** Wellbeing risk score, suggested action.

## 10. Plagiarism Detection for Assignments
- **Description:** Detect copied or AI-generated content in student assignment submissions.
- **Use Case:** Academic integrity enforcement.
- **Input:** Assignment text submissions.
- **Output:** Plagiarism probability, flagged submissions.

---

## Notes
- Each model can be built using Python (scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch, etc.) and integrated via REST API or microservice.
- Start with simple models (logistic regression, random forest) and iterate to more complex ones as data grows.
- Data privacy and ethical use should be considered for all models.
